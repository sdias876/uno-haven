Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026

In short: you can play without an account. If you sign up, we keep your email, your player name and your game progress so you can play on any device. Your email is only used for your account. We don't sell it, share it for marketing or send newsletters.

Playing without an account

Your name, picture, coins, level, stats and settings are saved in your browser on this device only. Nothing about you is sent to us. When you join a game, the other players see the name and picture you chose.

What we store if you sign up

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send account emails.
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way scrambled code (a hash). Nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Your player name and picture choice.
  • Your game progress: coins, level, wins, games played, best wins, spin-wheel and daily gift timing.

Coins are play money for fun only. They can't be bought, sold or cashed out.

Emails we send

Only emails about your account: a welcome email when you register, and a password-reset link when you ask for one. That's all.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no advertising trackers and no analytics cookies.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, or share it for marketing.
  • We don't ask for payment details.

Services that help run the game

The game runs on Render (hosting). Accounts are kept in an Upstash database. Account emails are sent by Brevo. These services handle the data only to run Ek Haven. They are based in the United States and the European Union.

Keeping it, and deleting it

We keep your account until you ask us to delete it. To delete your account or see what we hold about you, email support@sedigitaltransformation.com from the email address you signed up with. We'll take care of it within 30 days. Signing out removes your account from that device, but not from our database.

Children

Ek Haven is a family-friendly card and board game. Players under 13 should only create an account with a parent's or guardian's permission. A parent can ask us to delete a child's account at any time using the email above.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
